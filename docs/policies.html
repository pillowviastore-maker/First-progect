100-Night Risk-Free Trial

We want you to love your Pillowvia pillow. Sleep on it for up to 100 nights from the day it is delivered. If you're not completely satisfied, you can request a return for a full refund of the product price.

How to start a return

  1. Email us at pillowviastore@gmail.com within 100 days of delivery with your order number and the reason for your return.
  2. We will reply within 1–2 business days with return instructions and the return address. Items sent back without first requesting a return will not be accepted.
  3. Pack the pillow (with its cover) securely and ship it using a trackable service.

Conditions

  • One trial return per customer and per product.
  • The pillow must be returned with its cover and must not be damaged or heavily soiled.
  • Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please inspect your order on arrival. If your item is damaged, defective, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the issue, and we will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no extra cost to you.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you by email. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Please note that your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the refund.

Exchanges

Want a different color? Contact us and we'll be happy to help you exchange your pillow.

Order cancellations

You can cancel your order free of charge before it ships. Contact us as soon as possible at pillowviastore@gmail.com.

Questions

Contact us at pillowviastore@gmail.com — we reply within 24 hours on business days.
